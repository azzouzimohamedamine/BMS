\section{Limitations}
No ageing data, unverified nominal capacity, one cell, no temperature measurement, no measured SOC, constant-current tests only, confounded test history, in-sample EKF identification at 25\,°C, no gradient-boosting library other than scikit-learn's, NumPy recurrent networks of small size without extensive tuning, unverified literature entries and an unexecuted (stub-tested) dashboard. See \texttt{docs/LIMITATIONS.md}.
\section{Future research directions}
Multi-cell ageing data (CALCE or other public sets) with documented nominal capacity to enable SOH and RUL with uncertainty quantification; dynamic drive-cycle data (e.g.\ US06/FUDS) to test the SOC methods under realistic loads; temperature-dependent and SOC-dependent ECM parameters and adaptive EKF noise tuning; a measured temperature channel for over-temperature diagnosis; hyper-parameter optimisation of recurrent models with a standard deep-learning framework; protocol-aware anomaly detection.
\section{Conclusion}
Within what four single-cycle files can support, a reproducible BMS pipeline was built and SOC estimation was benchmarked across temperature, rate and noise: the EKF generalised best, trees excelled only at low current, and recurrent networks were not competitive. SOH, RUL and real fault diagnosis were shown to be infeasible with these data and are therefore guarded rather than faked.
